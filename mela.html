✦ ANNUAL WINTER CONFLUENCE • PAMBA RIVERBED ARENA

The Annual Ayroor Kathakali Mela

Every January, as cool winter breezes sweep across the sacred River Pamba, the expansive sandbanks at Cherukolpuzha (Sree Vidyadhiraja Nagar) transform into Asia's grandest open-air amphitheater for classical dance-theatre. For seven consecutive nights from dusk to dawn, the ancient epics awaken under the warm glow of traditional brass oil lamps.

SREE VIDYADHIRAJA NAGAR • CHERUKOLPUZHA

The Natural Sandbed Amphitheater

Unlike proscenium auditoriums with velvet seats, the Ayroor Kathakali Mela is experienced seated on the cool, soft river sand. Thousands of spectators from across Kerala, India, and overseas gather in collective reverence as the Kelikottu drums echo across the waters.

Organized by the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club and Natyabharathi, the festival brings together over 100 distinguished masters of Vesham (actors), vocal maestros (Ponnani & Singidi), and Chenda-Maddalam percussion virtuosos. Admission remains unconditionally free, upholding the democratic spirit of community art patronage.

7 Dusk-to-Dawn Nights

Each evening commences at 6:30 PM with Kelikottu and concludes at 6:00 AM as dawn breaks over the Pamba.

Complete Aattakatha Plays

Plays are staged in their unhurried, uncut classical entirety, preserving the subtle emotional depth of the Natyashastra.

Audience seated on Pamba Sandbanks • All-Night Performance
REPERTOIRE & DRAMATIC CYCLES

Classic Aattakathas of the Mela

Each year, the festival features masterworks of Malayalam classical literature written by Unnayi Warrier, Kottayathu Thampuran, and Irayimman Thampi.

EPIC CYCLE 01

Nalacharitham (Days 1–4)

Nalacharitham • Unnayi Warrier

The Shakespearean masterpiece of Kathakali, spanning the poignant romance, cosmic trials, and joyous reunion of King Nala and Princess Damayanti.

Sringara Paccha & Minukku Golden Swan
EPIC CYCLE 02

Kalyanasaugandhikam

Kalyanasaugandhikam • Kottayathu Thampuran

The celestial encounter between Bheemasena and Lord Hanuman in the Gandhamadana forests, showcasing devotional humility overcoming pride.

Vella Thadi Paccha Bhakti & Veera
EPIC CYCLE 03

Karnasapadham

Karnasapadham • Mali Madhavan Nair

The heart-wrenching emotional tragedy of Karna, confronted by his mother Kunti on the eve of the colossal Kurukshetra war.

Karuna Rasa Kathi Vesham Minukku (Kunti)
EPIC CYCLE 04

Duryodhanavadham

Duryodhanavadham • Vayaskara Aryan Narayanan Mooss

The ferocious climax of the Mahabharata, culminating in the titanic duel between Bheema and Dussasana, accompanied by thunderous Chenda beats.

Raudra Rasa Chuvanna Thadi Kathi
EPIC CYCLE 05

Kiratham

Kiratham • Irayimman Thampi

Arjuna’s penance for the divine Pasupatastra weapon and his confrontation with Lord Shiva appearing as a humble hunter (Kattala).

Karutha Thadi Arjuna Divine Boar Hunt
EPIC CYCLE 06

Dakshayagam & Subhadraharanam

Dakshayagam & Subhadraharanam • Irayimman Thampi & Thampuran

Grand mythological pageants depicting the celestial sacrifice of Daksha, the wrath of Veerabhadra, and romantic heroic abduction of Subhadra.

Tamas & Sattva Veerabhadra Grand Ensemble
SCHOLASTIC RIGOR & DIALOGUE

Morning Seminars & Kathakali Kacheri

While evenings belong to the performance, mornings from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM are dedicated to serious scholarly discourse, Cholliyattam demonstrations, and audience-artist dialogues.

01

Natya Sangeetha Kacheri

Demonstrations dedicated exclusively to Sopanam vocal ragas and rhythmic tala patterns, exploring their therapeutic and emotional properties.

02

Chutti & Aharya Workshops

Live demonstrations where master craftsmen explain the chemistry of natural mineral pigments, rice-paste mixing, and wooden crown carving.

03

Student Interactive Sessions

Hundreds of visiting high-school and university students engage directly with living legends to decode the 24 Hasthalakshana mudras.
