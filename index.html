INDIA'S FIRST KATHAKALI VILLAGE

A HERITAGE BEYOND GENERATIONS

Ayroor, situated along the sacred waters of the River Pamba in Pathanamthitta district, holds the rare distinction of being declared India's first 'Kathakali Gramam' (Kathakali Village). Since 1995, under the dedicated patronage of the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club, the village has nurtured this centuries-old classical art into an everyday communal rhythm.

01

Makeup & Chutti

The Sacred Greenroom Transformation

Hours of meditative craftsmanship in the Aniyara (greenroom). Natural stone pigments, purified rice paste, and layered paper contours (Chutti) sculpt divine beings and mythical demons.

02

Traditional Costumes

Udhukku, Kireedam & Imperial Silks

The monumental flared skirt (Udhukku), carved wooden crowns (Kireedam) inlaid with glass gems, shoulder bells, and golden ornaments weighing up to 30 kilograms.

03

Facial Expressions

The Nine Sentiments (Navarasas)

Mastery of the Navarasas—Shringara, Hasya, Karuna, Roudra, Veera, Bhayanaka, Bhibhatsa, Adbhuta, and Shanta—articulated through disciplined eye movements and facial muscles.

04

Hand Gestures

Hastha Mudras of the Epics

A codified silent vocabulary of 24 root mudras from the ancient Hastha Lakshana Deepika, capable of conveying thousands of philosophical concepts, emotions, and nature imagery.

05

The Performance

Attakatha Under the Vilakku

From twilight dusk until daybreak dawn, fueled by the resonant percussions of Chenda and Maddalam, illuminated only by the flickering golden flame of the bronze Aattuvilakku.

PRESERVING LIVING TRADITIONS

OUR CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS

Natyabharathi functions as a cultural sanctuary, bridging classical training with community education.

Natyabharathi Kathakali Centre

Established in 1995, delivering rigorous Gurukula training in Vesham, Kathakali Sangeetham, Chenda, Maddalam, and Chutti under Kerala Kalamandalam masters.

Traditional Koothambalam

An architecturally authentic wooden performance theater conceived according to Natyashastra acoustics, hosting temple performances and classical art recitals.

Natyabharathi Classical Library

A priceless archive housing rare palm-leaf manuscripts, historical Attakatha commentaries, Sanskrit dramaturgical texts, and photographic documentation.

Ayroor Kathakali Troupe

An internationally acclaimed touring company presenting full-scale Attakathas, lecture-demonstrations, and educational workshops worldwide.

ANNUAL SEVEN-DAY CULTURAL SPECTACLE

THE CELEBRATION OF A LIVING ART

Held on the Golden Sands of River Pamba (Pampa Manalpuram)

Every year, the riverbed of Ayroor transforms into a sea of art lovers. The Kathakali Mela features grand cultural processions (Shobhayathra), scholarly seminars, night-long performances, and national honours.

Shobhayathra Procession

Hundreds of villagers in traditional attire marching alongside Vesham artists, elephants, and percussion ensembles.

All-Night Attakathas

Continuous midnight performances by the greatest living Kathakali doyens under open starry skies.

National Art Seminars

Academic discourses, lecture demonstrations, and workshops conducted by leading Indologists and dramatists.
