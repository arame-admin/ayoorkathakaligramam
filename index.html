CULTURAL IDENTITY

The Village Crowned by Kathakali

Nestled on the serene banks of the river Pamba in Pathanamthitta district, Ayroor is uniquely distinguished across Kerala as "Kathakali Gramam"—a village dedicated to the eternal revival of this classical art form.

Here, the sublime synthesis of Natya, Geetha, and Vadhya is an everyday reality. For generations, the village has nurtured connoisseurs, patronized master artists, and preserved the sacred sanctity of Kathakali in its truest temple tradition.

Pamba Riverfront

The sacred shores provide the natural backdrop and sandbed amphitheater for grand traditional recitals.

Living Tradition

A generational continuum where every home resonates with the rhythms of classical Malayalam theatre.

ANGIKA ABHINAYA • HASTHA LAKSHANA DEEPIKA

The Sacred Mudras of Kathakali

In Kathakali, twenty-four root hand gestures form an eloquent sign language codified in the Hastalakshana Deepika. Through graceful combinations of fingers and palms, masters narrate epic sagas without uttering a single spoken word.

01 / 09
01 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 01

PATHAKA പതാക

SIGNIFICATION The Flag / Royal Splendor

Formed by bending the ring finger inwards to touch the thumb, while keeping the other fingers erect. In Kathakali, Pathaka represents the Sun, King, Elephant, Lion, Bull, Waves, Fire, Night, and Divine Benediction.

Sun King Elephant Fire Benediction
02 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 02

MUDRAAKHYAM മുദ്രാഖ്യം

SIGNIFICATION The Primal Seal / Signet

Formed by joining the tips of the index finger and the thumb into a ring with other fingers held straight. Signifies meditation (Dhyana), growth, ocean, sky, silence, and self-knowledge.

Meditation Ocean Cosmic Sky Silence Growth
03 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 03

KATAKAM കടകം

SIGNIFICATION The Bracelet / Golden Link

Formed with the index, middle, and ring fingers bent to touch the thumb while the little finger stays curved. Represents the sacred crown, mirror, female beauty, holding flowers, arrows, and peace.

Sacred Crown Mirror Flowers Arrow Peace
04 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 04

MUSHTI മുഷ്ടി

SIGNIFICATION The Fist / Martial Vigor

Formed by curling all four fingers tightly into the palm with the thumb held firmly over them. Denotes the charioteer, boundless strength, combat, bow grip, courage, and perseverance.

Charioteer Strength Combat Bow Grip Victory
05 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 05

KARTHARIMUKHAM കർത്തരീമുഖം

SIGNIFICATION Scissor’s Face

Formed by extending the index and little fingers while bending the middle and ring fingers towards the thumb. Depicts separation, palace spires, lightning, gentle creepers, arrows, and children.

Separation Lightning Palace Tower Creeper Child
06 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 06

SHUKATHUNDAM ശുകതുണ്ഡം

SIGNIFICATION Parrot’s Beak

Formed by bending the index finger like a hooked beak with the thumb pressed to its base while other fingers curl gracefully. Represents arrows, birds, sharp shooting weapons, mysteries, and stealth.

Piercing Arrow Parrot Divine Bird Mystery Weapon
07 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 07

KAPITHAKAM കപിത്ഥകം

SIGNIFICATION Wood Apple / Sacred Ring

Formed with the index and ring fingers curved and pressed with the thumb. Used to delineate nets, sacred feathers, lingering doubts, offerings to deities, and graceful birds.

Hunter Net Peacock Feather Sacred Offering Doubt
08 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 08

HAMSAPAKSHAM ഹംസപക്ഷം

SIGNIFICATION Swan’s Wing

Formed by stretching all fingers straight in a slight upward curve, thumb folded parallel. Represents the swan, celestial beings, gentle night breeze, the full moon, and dialogue addressing ‘You’ or ‘I’.

Swan Full Moon Night Breeze Deities Dialogue
09 / 09 • ASAMYUTHA HASTHA
ROOT MUDRA 09

SIKHARAM ശിഖരം

SIGNIFICATION The Peak / Pinnacle

Formed by holding the fingers closed in a fist while the thumb stands straight up like a pinnacle. Signifies mountain summits, wandering, thirst, the sounding horn, armor, and divine protection.

Mountain Peak Wandering Thirst Sacred Horn Divine Refuge
ANNUAL CONVERGENCE

Ayroor Kathakali Mela

A week-long pilgrimage of art under starlit skies on the sands of the sacred Pamba.

Every year, the sandy riverbed of the Pamba transforms into Kerala’s largest open-air Kathakali arena. Lit by traditional bronze Nilavilakku lamps and the canopy of stars, thousands gather from across the world to witness all-night performances by the finest masters of the art.

AYROOR KATHAKALI MELA 2027 • 7-DAY FESTIVAL SCHEDULE

Seven Nights of Sacred Theatre

From January 18 to 24, 2027, the sacred sandbanks of River Pamba transform into Kerala’s grandest Kathakali arena. Explore the all-night schedule of legendary Aattakatha performances by celebrated masters.

DAY 01 / 07
  • DAY 01 • 18 JAN 2027
    6:00 PM – 4:00 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    KOTTAYAM THAMPURAN PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    KALYANASOUGANDHIKAM

    കല്യാണസൗഗന്ധികം
    THEME & EPISODE

    The Quest for Sougandhika & Fraternal Meeting of Bheema and Hanuman

    On the inaugural night of Ayroor 2027, Kottayam Thampuran's timeless epic opens the festival. Bheemasena sets out to retrieve the celestial fragrant lotus for Panchali, encountering the wise and mighty Hanuman disguised on the Gandhamadana mountain.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Bheema (Paccha) Hanuman (Vellathadi) Panchali (Minukku)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Traditional Keli, Purappadu & Melappadam • Chenda, Maddalam, Chengila, Elathalam

  • DAY 02 • 19 JAN 2027
    6:30 PM – 5:00 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    UNNAYI WARRIER PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    NALACHARITHAM (DAY 1)

    നളചരിതം (ഒന്നാം ദിവസം)
    THEME & EPISODE

    The Golden Swan’s Divine Embassy of Romance

    Unnayi Warrier’s crowning jewel of classical Malayalam literature. King Nala wanders the palace groves of Nishadha and catches the golden swan, who promises to fly to Vidarbha as a celestial messenger of love to princess Damayanthi.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Nala (Paccha) Hamsam (Minukku) Damayanthi (Minukku) Narada (Minukku)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Soulful Sopana Sangeetham • Classical Ponnani and Singidi Vocal Duet

  • DAY 03 • 20 JAN 2027
    6:30 PM – 5:30 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    VAYASKARA MOOSS PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    DURYODHANA VADHAM

    ദുര്യോധനവധം
    THEME & EPISODE

    The Climax of Kurukshetra & Vengeance of Bheema

    Vayaskara Mooss's monumental all-night drama portraying the final hours of the Kurukshetra war. Features thunderous Chenda rhythms, the diplomatic embassy of Lord Krishna, and the ferocious climax fulfilling the sacred vow of Panchali.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Raudra Bheema Duryodhana (Kathi) Dussasana (Chuvannathadi) Krishna (Paccha)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Thunderous Asura Vadyam • High-intensity Chenda Melam during Thiranokku & Yuddham

  • DAY 04 • 21 JAN 2027
    6:30 PM – 4:30 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    MALI MADHAVAN NAIR PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    KARNASHAPATHAM

    കർണ്ണശപഥം
    THEME & EPISODE

    The Tragedy of Karna & The Maternal Confrontation at Dusk

    Mali Madhavan Nair’s deeply moving emotional masterpiece. On the sunset shores of the Ganges, Queen Kunti discloses to the warrior Karna the secret of his divine lineage, setting in motion an agonizing conflict between honor, fate, and friendship.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Karna (Kathi) Kunti (Minukku) Duryodhana (Kathi) Bhanumathi (Minukku)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Subtle Maddalam and Edakka accompaniment for poignant Karuna and Shringara Rasas

  • DAY 05 • 22 JAN 2027
    6:30 PM – 5:00 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    IRAYIMMAN THAMPI PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    KIRATHAM

    കിരാതം
    THEME & EPISODE

    Arjuna’s Penance & The Celestial Hunter’s Boon

    Irayimman Thampi’s dramatic retelling of Arjuna’s penance in the high Himalayas. Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvathi appear in tribal hunter guise (Kiratha & Kattalathi) to test the warrior's humility before bestowing the divine Pasupatastra weapon.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Arjuna (Paccha) Kiratha / Shiva (Hunter) Kattalathi / Parvathi Mookasura
    ORCHESTRATION

    Fierce forest battle rhythms • Dynamic Chenda-Maddalam Jugalbandi

  • DAY 06 • 23 JAN 2027
    6:30 PM – 5:30 AM • ALL-NIGHT
    IRAYIMMAN THAMPI PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    DAKSHAYAGAM

    ദക്ഷയാഗം
    THEME & EPISODE

    The Cosmic Fury of Veerabhadra & Fall of Pride

    The arrogance of Prajapati Daksha and the tragic sacrifice of Sati. Lord Shiva’s grief summons the terrifying Veerabhadra and Bhadrakali to destroy the grand sacrifice, demonstrating the ultimate transcendence of divine righteousness.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Daksha (Kathi) Veerabhadra (Chuvannathadi) Bhadrakali (Kari) Sati (Minukku)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Monumental Thiranokku percussion • Massive triple-Chenda invocation

  • DAY 07 • 24 JAN 2027
    GRAND FINALE • 6:00 PM – SUNRISE
    AMRITASHASTRI & ITTIRARICHA MENON PAMBA SANDBANKS AMPHITHEATER

    LAVANASURAVADHAM & SANTHANAGOPALAM

    ലവണാസുരവധം & സന്താനഗോപാലം
    THEME & EPISODE

    Divine Reunion, Universal Grace & Consecration at Dawn

    The grand concluding night features the reconciliation of Rama with his twin sons Lava and Kusa, followed by Arjuna and Krishna’s cosmic voyage to Vaikuntha. Concludes at sunrise on the Pamba riverbed with the sacred Dhanaasi benediction.

    PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS
    Sree Rama (Paccha) Sree Krishna (Paccha) Lava & Kusa (Minukku) Vipra (Minukku)
    ORCHESTRATION

    Traditional Mangala Keli & Dawn Dhanaasi • Grand community ovation

Kalari & Guru-Sishya

TRADITIONAL ACOUSTIC ENSEMBLE

The Sacred Instruments of Kathakali

The drama, cadence, and spiritual fervor of Kathakali are ignited by Kerala’s classical percussion ensemble. Explore the legendary instruments that breathe life into every performance.

01 / 04
ASURA VADHYAM

Chenda ചെണ്ട

The Roar of Classical Drama

Dominating heroic (Veera) and ferocious (Raudra) duel sequences, commanding the entire amphitheater with high-amplitude reverberations.

DEVA VADHYAM

Maddalam മദ്ദളം

The Divine Base Resonance

Tied around the waist and struck with both hands using rice-paste finger caps, providing the deep tonal foundation that grounds every abhinaya.

GHANA VADHYAM

Chengila & Ilathalam ചെങ്ങില • ഇലത്താളം

The Golden Keepers of Metric Tala

The acoustic compass of Kathakali Sangeetham. Ponani singer strikes the bronze gong while Singidi clashes cymbals to govern tempo.

SOPANA VADHYAM

Idakka ഇടയ്ക്ക

The Sacred Melodic Hourglass

The sanctified drum of Sopanam tradition, accompanying female character roles (Sthreevesham) with delicate melodic notes across two octaves.
