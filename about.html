The Heritage of Ayroor Kathakali Gramam

Nestled on the serene, sacred banks of River Pamba in Pathanamthitta district, Central Kerala, Ayroor holds the singular distinction of being the first village in India officially named after a classical performing art form. With over 70% of its populace versed in Kathakali literacy, this riverside sanctuary preserves an unbroken, living continuum of temple theatre tradition.

COMMUNITY MOVEMENT & OFFICIAL SANCTION

The Journey to Kathakali Gramam

The transformation of Ayroor into a global cultural landmark was not an overnight government decree, but a grassroots renaissance spearheaded by connoisseurs, teachers, and villagers spanning over three decades.

The movement took formal root in 1987 with the establishment of the Kathakali Appreciation Forum, which subsequently expanded into the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club in 1995. Following a decade of sustained cultural activism and legislative resolutions, the village received formal approval from the Union Ministry of Home Affairs and the Surveyor General of India, renaming the revenue village as Ayroor Kathakali Gramam.

CULTURAL PEDAGOGY • UNIQUE SOCIAL CONSCIOUSNESS

70% Kathakali Literacy Rate

In Ayroor, Kathakali is not a bourgeois museum relic; it is an everyday language understood across generations, farmers, schoolteachers, and children alike.

01

School Kalari Clubs

Every educational institution across the village incorporates Kathakali clubs where students are initiated into hand gestures (Mudras), eye movements (Netra Abhinaya), and basic rhythm talas from primary grades.

02

Community Connoisseurship

Over 70% of the local population can distinguish between classical Aattakathas, decipher Sanskrit and Malayalam verses, and evaluate subtle differences between Paccha and Kathi performances without printed guides.

03

Innovative Productions

The Gramam has gained worldwide acclaim for pioneering inclusive adaptations, staging biblical parables such as The Prodigal Son and world classics like Don Quixote in classical Kathakali format.

ACADEMY OF CLASSICAL PERFORMING ARTS • ESTD. 1995

Natyabharathi Kathakali Centre

Established in 1995 under the direct tutelage of the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club, Natyabharathi Kathakali Centre is a singular cultural academy in Central Travancore. Nestled on the sacred sandbanks of River Pamba at Ayroor-Cherukolpuzha, the academy imparts intensive daily Kalari training to more than 120 young students across five core disciplines.

KALARI CURRICULUM

Core Classical Disciplines Taught

🎭

Kathakali Vesham

Intensive training in Angika Abhinaya, Meyyurappu (body flexibility), Chuzhippu, Navarasa expressions, Netra Sadhakam (eye movement exercises), 24 root Mudras, and stage choreography across Paccha, Kathi, Minukku, Kari, and Thadi characters.

🪕

Kathakali Sangeetham

Specialized classical vocal instruction rooted in traditional Sopana Sangeetham and Carnatic ragas. Students master Ponnani (lead vocal) with Chengila, Singidi (accompanying vocal) with Ilathalam, and raga-bhava theatrical delivery.

🥁

Kathakali Chenda

Mastery of Kerala's Asura Vadyam percussion. Rigorous training in wrist dexterity, stick work, classical Talas (Chembada, Adantha, Champa, Panchari, Thriputa), and dramatic stage accompaniment for heroic and wrathful characters.

🎨

Kathakali Chutti & Aharyam

The sacred ritual art of green-room costuming and facial makeup: applying lime and rice-paste ridges (Chutti), grinding natural mineral pigments (Manayola, Chayilyam), Kireedam crafting, and royal Aharyam costume drapery.

💃

Mohiniyattam & Classical Dance

Lyrical Lasya training focusing on graceful torso swaying (Andolika), delicate Mudra gestures, rhythmic Adavus, and authentic choreographies preserving Kerala's traditional classical dance heritage under Kalamandalam faculty.

🎓

Kalamandalam Feeder Channel

Students who successfully complete their foundation course at Natyabharathi are systematically recommended and enrolled at Kerala Kalamandalam Deemed University for collegiate diplomas, bachelor's, and postgraduate degrees in performing arts.

AFFILIATED WINGS, ARCHIVES & SACRED THEATRE

Institutions of Ayroor Kathakali Gramam

Beyond daily classroom training, the cultural ecosystem of Ayroor encompasses the first regional off-campus of Kerala Kalamandalam, a specialized classical library, an internationally touring resident troupe, and the historic initiative of an open-air Natyashastra Koothambalam on the banks of River Pamba.

✦ FIRST DEEMED UNIVERSITY OFF-CAMPUS • ESTD. 2014

Kerala Kalamandalam Off-Campus Centre

Inaugurated during the valedictory session of Kathakali Mela 2014 by Minister for Cultural Affairs Sri K. C. Joseph in the august presence of Sri P. N. Suresh (Vice-Chancellor), this institution holds the distinction of being adjudged as the very first off-campus training sub-centre of Kerala Kalamandalam Deemed University for Art and Culture. Over 100 students undergo professional instruction in Kathakali Vesham, Chenda, and Mohiniyattam under deputed Kalamandalam Gurus.

📜 Certification: 'Art Appreciation Certificate' conferred directly by Kerala Kalamandalam Deemed University
👥 Eligibility: Open to youth aged 12–20 irrespective of gender • Subsidized tuition fee of ₹750/month
📰 Admissions: Annual statewide notifications published in April; classes commence end of April
✦ KERALA GRANDHASALA SANGAM AFFILIATED • ESTD. 2010

Natyabharathi Classical Library

Started in 2010 under the auspices of the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club and affiliated with the Kerala Grandhasala Sangam, this specialized reference library is an invaluable boon to art historians and doctoral research fellows. The library houses an extraordinary repository of classical Kathakali literature, hand-inscribed Aattakathas, rare palm-leaf manuscripts, Tala treatises, and performance commentaries.

📚 Holdings: Comprehensive Kathakali commentaries, Sanskrit aesthetics, and Aattakatha manuscripts
🏛️ Governance: P. P. Ramachandran Pillai (President) • M. R. Venu (Secretary) • Jaya Kumari (Librarian)
🔍 Scholar Access: Open for university research fellows, scholars, and visiting international cultural delegates
✦ 20 PROFESSIONAL ARTISTS • EXPERIMENTAL PRODUCTIONS

Ayroor Resident Kathakali Troupe

The Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club maintains its own elite performing troupe of 20 accomplished artists across Vesham, vocal music, Chenda, Maddalam, and Chutti. Beyond traditional temple festivals, the troupe regularly conducts workshops and curriculum-aligned lecture-demonstrations in schools and universities. The troupe has gained international renown for revolutionary adaptations codified and rehearsed (*cholliyattam*) at Natyabharathi:

📖 Biblical Aattakathas: Groundbreaking Kathakali adaptations of Abrahaminte Bali (Sacrifice of Abraham) and Mudiyanaya Puthran (The Prodigal Son)
✍️ Poetry Choreography: Classical Kathakali staging of modern poems Kurathi and Kattalan by legendary poet late Kadammanitta Ramakrishnan
🎭 Troupe Management: Kalamandalam Arun (Troupe Manager) • Crew: Aranmula Omanakuttan, Ayroor Pradeep
✦ BHARATA'S NATYASHASTRA • ₹2 CRORE SECULAR THEATRE

The Monumental Pamba Koothambalam

A Koothambalam is a sacred traditional theatre designed for staging ancient dramatic art forms like Koodiyattam, Koothu, and Kathakali. In all of Kerala, there exist only fourteen traditional Koothambalams (twelve inside temple compounds and only two outside). In a historic cultural departure, the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club resolved to build a full-fledged, traditional Koothambalam outside the traditional temple confines, directly on the banks of River Pamba at Ayroor Kathakali Gramam.

📐 Architectural Fidelity: Strictly adhering to the sacred dimensions, acoustic resonance, and pillar geometry codified in Sage Bharata's Natyashastra
🌍 Secular Democratization: Built away from temple restrictions so art connoisseurs and scholars worldwide, regardless of religion or caste, can experience classical theatre
🤝 Patron Appeal: Projected outlay of ₹2 Crore; donations welcomed via the Koothambalam Construction Fund
