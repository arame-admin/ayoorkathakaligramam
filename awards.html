TRADITION & ENDOWMENTS

The Four Pillars of Recognition

Instituted under the auspices of the Pathanamthitta District Kathakali Club, these four canonical citations celebrate the diverse arts that together bring classical Kathakali theatre to life on the river sandbanks.

👑
Natyabharathi Award

The apex puraskaram instituted in 2000, presented to living masters for monumental lifetime achievement in Vesham, Sangeetham, Melam, and Chutti. Includes royal silk ponnada, bronze Nataraja statuette, and citation.

📜
Thonnakkal Kochupillai Award

Instituted in reverence of the illustrious scholar-actor Thonnakkal Kochupillai Panicker. Honors exceptional playwrights, researchers, Sanskrit commentators, and historians who preserve Attakkatha literature.

🎶
Thonnakkal Sangeetharatnam Award

Dedicated to classical vocal excellence and stage acoustics. Bestowed upon brilliant Kathakali and Sopanam vocalists whose raga-bhava interpretations elevate the dramatic pathos of the performance.

🏵️
O. M. S. Gopalakrishnan Nair Award

Commemorating visionary organizer and patron O. M. S. Gopalakrishnan Nair. Awarded for exemplary cultural leadership, arts journalism, literary criticism, and community service to the heritage of Kerala.
