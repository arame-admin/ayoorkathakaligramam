REGIONAL ACCESS & TRANSPORTATION

How to Reach Ayroor Kathakali Gramam

Conveniently situated in Central Travancore, Ayroor is well connected by rail, road, and international airports.

BY RAILWAY

Railway Access

• Chengannur Railway Station (CNGR): ~26 km (Major southern railway hub with express trains from all major Indian cities).

• Tiruvalla Railway Station (TRVL): ~30 km.
Taxis and KSRTC buses run frequently from both stations to Ayroor via Kozhencherry.

BY AIR

Airport Connectivity

• Cochin International Airport (COK): ~124 km (approx. 3.5 hours drive via MC Road).

• Trivandrum International Airport (TRV): ~115 km (approx. 3 hours drive via MC Road).

BY ROAD

Highway Routes

Accessible via Main Central (MC) Road (SH 1) branching off at Chengannur or Thiruvalla, and via Main Eastern Highway (SH 8) connecting through Ranni and Kozhencherry.

✦ VISITING ETIQUETTE & CODE OF REVERENCE

Preserving the Sanctity of the Sandbeds

Kathakali is revered as an act of prayer and spiritual dedication. We kindly request all visitors and scholars to honor traditional decorum.

01

Quiet Decorum during Invocations

Please maintain respectful silence during the opening Kelikottu, the lighting of the brass oil lamp (Aattavilakku), and the opening Purappadu dance.

02

Sandbed Seating Etiquette

Footwear is prohibited upon the performance stage and in the immediate oil-lamp perimeter. Connoisseurs sit together on the river sandbed.

03

Green Room Photography

Photography in the green room (Aniyara) is permitted only with prior approval from the Gramam coordination desk so as not to disturb the actor's meditation.
